#Microhabitat (2017)
**Lead Actress:** *Esom as Mi-so*
**Genre:** Drama | **Country:** South Korea
--- 
##Summary 
*Microhabitat* is a **Korean Film** about *Mi-so* , a woman who gives up her apartment to keep enjoying her favorite things - **whiskey, cigarretes,** and **freedom**.
It shows how she visits old friends while trying to live life her own way.
> "To live the life I want, I'll give up the things they say i need."
##Why I Like It
- The story feels **simple but deep**.
- *Mi-so* shows courage to live freely.
- The film's look and music make it **calm and emotional**.
